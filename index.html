Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Discord account?

Yes. Ace's Apex memberships provide access to the Ace's Apex Discord community and its membership features.

Which payment method should I use?

Indian members use INR payment options. International members use USD payment options. The available checkout option will be shown when purchasing.

How long does membership last?

Race Pass follows the applicable race-period access. Monthly Supporter is 30 days and Yearly Supporter is 365 days.

Who do I contact for support?

Please use the Ace's Apex Discord community's support channel or the support contact provided at checkout.

Are memberships refundable?

Refunds and cancellations are handled according to the Refund & Cancellation Policy below.

Policies & support

Clear policies are provided so members know what they are purchasing and how support is handled.

Terms & Conditions

Membership grants access to the Ace's Apex Discord community and applicable membership features for the purchased period. Memberships are personal and may not be resold or transferred. Community access remains subject to the Discord community rules.

Refund & Cancellation

If you experience a payment or access problem, contact Ace's Apex support with your payment details. Refund requests are reviewed individually. A refund may be issued for a duplicate or failed transaction where appropriate; completed access may be ended when a refund is processed.

Privacy

Ace's Apex uses information needed to provide membership access, identify payments, communicate with members, and operate the prediction league. Payment credentials are handled by the payment provider rather than stored by Ace's Apex.
